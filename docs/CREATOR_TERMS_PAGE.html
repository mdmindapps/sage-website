Sage Creator Program — Terms

The simple terms for promoting Sage as a creator. By joining the program (creating your affiliate account), you agree to them.

1Your commission

You earn 25% of every payment made by subscribers you refer — recurring, month after month, for up to 12 months per subscriber. No cap on how many people you refer.

2How referrals are tracked

You get a unique link. Anyone who installs Sage through it is tagged to you automatically (via RevenueCat + Insert Affiliate). Only genuine referrals count — you can't refer yourself or people who are already Sage subscribers.

3Payments

We pay weekly, on commissions that have cleared a short confirmation window (so refunds are already accounted for). You set up payment when you join. You're responsible for declaring and paying any taxes on your earnings in your own country.

4Disclosure

You must clearly disclose that you're an affiliate/partner whenever you promote Sage. It can be light — it just needs to be there. This protects you and us. Here's the easiest way on each platform:

Simplest — add #ad or "affiliate link" in the caption / description.
TikTok — More options → Disclose post content → turn on Branded content.
Instagram — Advanced settings → Add paid partnership label.
YouTube — Video details → Show more → check Paid promotion.
Facebook — use the Branded content tool / tag as a paid partnership.

5Please don't

  • Generate fake, bot, or incentivized installs or subscriptions
  • Make misleading claims, or any medical/health guarantees about results
  • Run paid ads that bid on "Sage" brand keywords, or spam links
  • Impersonate Sage or claim to officially speak for us

6Your content, your voice

You keep full creative control — just be honest. We may ask you to stop using a specific piece of content or to remove Sage links from anything misleading.

7Ending the program

Non-exclusive — either side can end it anytime. Commissions already confirmed still get paid. If it ends due to a breach (e.g. fraud), pending unconfirmed commissions may be voided.

8Independent relationship

You're an independent creator, not an employee or agent of Sage.

9Changes

We may update these terms and will notify active creators of anything material.

Questions about anything here? Just reach out — we're happy to walk you through it.
